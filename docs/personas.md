# Personas

Who the web app's screens are written for, and the questions each screen must answer for them. Stories are spec #1's numbers. None of these people are real.

## Ruth, the giver

64, grandmother. Holds a little Bitcoin in a phone wallet her son set up; has never used any other crypto app. Starts the jar, invites the guardians, sets the rule, tops it up. Until handover the jar sits in her account (the family account).

- Do I need to install or learn anything? (1, 2, 3)
- What exactly do I have to type, and what happens if I get it wrong? (4–9)
- Who can take the money, and when? (10, 35, 40)
- How do I put Bitcoin in? (13, 15)
- Can I stop a bad emergency payment? (38)

## Priya, a guardian

41, the child's aunt. Gets an invite link from Ruth. Not a Bitcoin user.

- What am I agreeing to, and what will I have to do? (10, 32, 35, 47)
- Can I take money out? (40, 40a)
- Someone asked for an emergency payment: what do I approve? (33–37)
- The child says it's their phone: how do I know? (46, 47, 48)

## Sunny, the child

17, has their own phone. Gets a "Set up my jar" link near the unlock date.

- What do I do, and what do I need to keep? (41–43)
- Who else can use my jar afterwards? (44, 50, 54)
- What if I lose my phone? (43, 54)
- Once it's mine, how do I send or give it away? (55, 56)

## Leo, a new holder

19, Sunny's friend, receiving the jar as a gift. Has his own phone.

- What do I give Sunny so the jar comes to me? (56, 58a)
- Does Sunny keep any way back to the Bitcoin? (57, 59)

## Kai, the sceptic

A relative, or a hackathon judge, who believes nothing the app says until they can check it.

- Is this real Bitcoin on a real chain? (68, 69, 71)
- What happened to this jar, refusals included? (69, 70)
- Can I see the rule say no myself? (36, 40a, 52)
