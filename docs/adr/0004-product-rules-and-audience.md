# Product rules and who Sats Jar is for

Sats Jar is for families where at least one adult already holds Bitcoin. A jar is filled only with Bitcoin, sent from any Bitcoin wallet or exchange app by scanning the jar's QR code; a relative with no Bitcoin cannot fill a jar in this version ("top up with any coin" is the README's next step, not built). The parent registers a Bitcoin address they control for the child, and we say so plainly (R14). We never hold anyone's Bitcoin keys and we do not build a wallet.

Every design answer follows three rules:

1. **Users see Bitcoin, never NEAR.** No account names, keys, seed phrases, gas, signing prompts or wallet pop-ups.
2. **Anyone can always check the proof.** Every jar has a "see how it works" view: the jar NFT, its Bitcoin address, the NEAR and Bitcoin transactions, and the refused attacks, with explorer links.
3. **Hide complexity, never hide trust.** Nothing behind the scenes may hold keys that move money outside the rule (R2), and nothing on the demo path is mocked.

When a question is open, we pick what makes the NFT and its rule plain and checkable: the NFT visibly does something, proof beats promise, the feared attacks fail on camera, the rule leads and the plumbing follows, and small and finished beats big and mocked.
