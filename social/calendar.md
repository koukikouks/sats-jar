# Sats Jar on X: launch week (14 to 20 Nov 2026)

Drafts only. Nothing here is scheduled or posted except the teaser draft already in Postiz.

## How these follow the marketing skills

These choices come from Corey Haines' `social` and `launch` skills (coreyhaines31/marketingskills), including the skill's notes on the X ranking code:

- **The hook is in the first eight words, and it says something specific.** X scores how long people stay on a post, so the opening has to hold them.
- **Links go in the first reply, never in the post.** Links in the body cut reach. Postiz also strips links from X posts, so you add each link reply by hand after the post is live.
- **Videos run longer than 8 seconds.** X only gives video credit above a minimum length. Every clip and animation here is 10 to 54 seconds.
- **Two posts a day, at least a few hours apart.** Posts from the same account close together compete with each other.
- **Write for "send this to a friend".** X scores shares by DM separately. Most posts here are written so a parent or a Bitcoiner would forward them to someone.
- **Stay for the first 30 minutes after each post.** Early replies count, so pick times when someone can answer.
- **No AI tells:** no "it's not X, it's Y", no em dashes, no one-line-per-sentence posts, no "Thoughts?".
- **Profile clicks are scored,** so the header and the pinned post matter. The launch video becomes the pinned post.

Pillars for the week: the story (family, a gift that grows up), the proof (refusals, zero keys, open record), how it works (rules, top-ups, handover), and building in public (roadmap, crayons, honest gaps).
