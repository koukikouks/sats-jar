# Sats Jar story video: storyboard and script (v1, sketch)

About 38 seconds. Crayon people first, so we can sketch it out, then take it from there.
Human-forward: one family, four moments in one life. A small jar in the corner of every
scene fills up as the years go by, so the idea ("it grows with you") is shown, not said.

**Vocabulary.** "Allowance" is out. The video says **monthly gift** (alternatives to try:
*monthly boost*, *a little help*). The graduation payoff is **"Your jar is yours now"**, not
"your first allowance": in the product the monthly payments run during college, and graduation
is the handover, when the jar and everything in it becomes theirs alone. That is both more
accurate and a bigger moment.

**Tagline (pick one).**

1. **Savings that grow with you.** (recommended) Sub-line: *There for every step.*
2. A gift that grows up with them.
3. Grows with you. There for every step.
4. A little every month. A lot over a lifetime.

> Savings that grow with you. Sats Jar.

## Notes

- **Name.** "Sats Jar" is a placeholder in the end card until the name is settled.
- **Honesty.** The demo runs on testnet, so the end card carries that line. The video shows the
  idea, not a real jar: nothing in it is a real balance or a real transaction.
- **Claims to keep true.** Anyone with Bitcoin can add to a jar (a relative with none cannot yet).
  The jar pays a capped amount until handover, and handover is confirmed by guardians, so it is
  not instant in real life. The video compresses that into one moment.
- **Next.** Once the sketch feels right: swap in the final name, record the voice-over, add a
  gentle music bed, and decide whether to animate the key moments (the coin arc, the gasp, the hug).
