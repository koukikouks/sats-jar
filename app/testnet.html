Testnet and demo timing

It runs on NEAR testnet and Bitcoin testnet3. The coins are test coins with no value, so please don't send real Bitcoin to a jar.

Time is shortened for the demo. A period and the unlock date are minutes or hours, not months and years, and wherever a jar's timing shows it is labelled Demo timing.
