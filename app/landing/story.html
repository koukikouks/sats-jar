The story

One jar, from day one to eighteen.

Grandpa starts a jar the day she's born. Everyone who loves her drops coins in, nobody can raid it, and at eighteen it's all hers.

Make a jar See how it works
What happens in the video
  1. Day 1. The day she's born, Grandpa makes her a jar and drops in one coin: "This one's for later."
  2. The rule. A note tied to the jar: a little each month, all of it at 18, and nobody can change it. "Not even me."
  3. Ages 1, 5 and 7. First steps, first day of school, a lost tooth. Each time, someone drops a coin in.
  4. Age 9. Money gets tight at home. The jar is stamped "The rule says no": nobody can touch it.
  5. Age 12. "Your monthly gift arrived!" It buys her a sketchbook.
  6. Age 16. Grandpa has moved into a care home, but a birthday coin still arrives: "love, Grandpa".
  7. Age 18. "The jar is yours now." It's full to the brim, with everyone's names around it, and Grandpa's first coin right at the bottom.
  8. Some gifts grow up with you. Start a jar for someone you love.

This is a testnet demo. It runs on Bitcoin's test network. Test coins have no value, so please don't send real Bitcoin to a jar.
